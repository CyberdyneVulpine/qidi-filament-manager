Backups

Adds, edits, removals, hides, and restores stay staged until Apply Changes. That saves one backup, updates the load menu, and reloads Klipper.

Klipper shuts down if the touchscreen selects a material that was not in the catalog when Klipper last started. Apply Changes before choosing a new, renamed, or restored material.

This page is open on the local network, with no extra login, the same way Fluidd and Moonraker are on a stock QIDI. Do not forward the port to the internet.
